The cast

The first fourteen bodies are outlines with a face. The cast are someones: each has parts that move on their own, a temperament, a register, and in some cases a second body it becomes.

Who they are

::table Creature | What it is | Temperament | Parts and habits `fox` | The sly one: tall ears, cheek fluff, a brush of a tail with a pale tip | `sly` (smug, looks away) | tail that trails behind every move `pangolin` | The guardian: plated armour in a smooth material | `stoic` | plates; rolls into a ball on error and to sleep `owl` | The scholar: brow tufts, big eyes, folded wings | `scholar` (slow blinks, no hopping) | its head turns right round while it thinks `axolotl` | The support agent: pink, blushing, gills | `curious` | frills that fan out when it listens and droop when it is sad `jelly` | The ambient companion: a glass bell | `dreamy` (floats) | tendrils that hang, sway and lag `moth` | The night companion: fuzzy, dazed | `drawn` (floats) | wings that flutter while it thinks, feathery antennae `sprout` | The one that grows: seed, hatchling, grown | `eager` | a lifecycle blended by `age`; a sprig that grows `octo` | The orchestrator: eight arms | `busy` | an arm lights up for every running tool (`observe('tool')`) `toaster` | The appliance | `perky` | a slot, a lever, and toast that pops on success `snail` | The patient one: eyes on stalks, a shell | `patient` (slow) | retreats into its shell on error and to sleep `comet` | The fast-mode mascot: a lantern star | `showOff` (spins) | a streak that lengthens with speed `swarm` | The crew: dots that gather into one shape | `hive` (floats) | the `swarm` material; scatters when it is low `orb` | The serious family: a glass sphere | `serene` (floats, still) | no face until it speaks, reacts or looks (`faceOn: 'talk'`) `glyph` | The monochrome line family | `precise` (snap blinks) | the `line` material: an outline in its colour, light on dark pages ::end

Use them like any type: <bot-avatar type="owl">, createBot(el, { type: 'octo' }). Each brings its own defaults (the jelly is glass, the comet a lantern, the glyph a line); anything you set wins. identity keeps the draw it always had, so an id keeps its bot (an id that drew a retired shape gets that shape's successor).

Parts

A part is drawn round the body in one of three layers (painted on it, behind it, in front of it) and moves from the pose: trailing parts lag with a spring the simulation runs, wings and frills beat faster when the bot is busy or airborne, arms light up with running tools, a popup rises on success, a shell shrinks away as the body morphs. The kinds: tail, tendrils, wings, frills, arms, shell, plates, slot, popup, sprig, knob, spiral, streak, beak. Parts are plain data on the creature, so they travel to the drawing threads and into exports.

Bodies that change

A creature can have another outline it becomes in some states (morph): the pangolin's ball, the snail's shell. The body blends between the two over a moment, keeping its fur. A creature can also have a lifecycle (stages): the sprout is a seed at age: 0, a hatchling at 0.5, grown at 1. Age is yours to keep (a count of sessions, days since it hatched); pass it back and it grows.

Your own creature

The built-in cast lives in src/creatures/, one module each, and registerCreature() takes the same format:

```js import { registerCreature, geometry } from '@kucukkanat/bots'; const { circle, poly, polarUnion, chaikin } = geometry; registerCreature({ type: 'newt', label: 'Newt', color: '#7BC9A5', faceY: 0.1, outline: () => chaikin(polarUnion([circle(0, 0.1, 0.8), poly([[-0.9, 0.4], [-0.2, 0.9], [-0.9, 0.9]])]), 2), extras: { parts: [{ kind: 'tail', anchor: [0.5, 0.6], side: 1, len: 0.9, width: 0.22 }] }, temperament: 'nervous', // or bring one: temperaments: { skittish: { motion: {…}, face: {…}, shy: 1 } } defaults: { eyeStyle: 'dot', shading: 'smooth' }, morph: { states: ['sleeping'], outline: () => polarUnion([circle(0, 0.05, 0.85)]) }, // curled up to sleep }); createBot('#x', { type: 'newt' }); ```

Outlines are closed point lists in a box of about −1..1 (y down), resampled to 200 points from the top centre, so a morph outline lines up with the main one. Habits a temperament can carry: aloof, shy, float, snap, showOff, swivel.
